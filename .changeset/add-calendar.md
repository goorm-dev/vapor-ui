Add `Calendar`: a month view for picking one date or a date range (`mode="range"`), built without date dependencies. `Calendar.Root` takes `value`/`defaultValue`/`onValueChange`, `defaultMonth`, `locale` (default `ko-KR`), `weekStartsOn`, and `disabled`; `Calendar.Header` and `Calendar.Grid` assemble the default layout, and `*Primitive` parts allow custom compositions.
