Add the `SideNavigation` compound component for sidebar menus. `Link` is a top-level page, `Group` with `Trigger` and `Panel` opens a list of `SubLink` pages under its header, and `Link` and `SubLink` take `current` and `disabled`. Each group opens and closes on its own and supports `open`, `defaultOpen`, and `onOpenChange`.
